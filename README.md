# OpenChain-Business-Operations Study Group

The OpenChain Business Operations is a community forum for exploring new ideas, identifying emerging requirements, and discussing potential projects that can help shape the direction of the OpenChain Project. 

## Goals

- Establish Operational Compliance best practices
- Launch practical delivery work to provide Operational Compliance guidance
- Align new topics with existing OpenChain standards (ISO/IEC 5230 & ISO/IEC 18974)
