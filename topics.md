# OpenChain Business Operations Study Group Backlog

This backlog is used to track ideas, discussion topics, emerging requirements, and potential work items for the OpenChain Business Operations Study Group.

## Purpose

The Business Operations Study Group serves as a community forum for:

- Exploring new ideas
- Identifying emerging operational requirements
- Discussing potential projects
- Sharing best practices
- Evaluating opportunities for OpenChain collaboration

Inclusion in this backlog does not imply approval, prioritization, or commitment to a future project.

---

## Topic Lifecycle

```text
Idea → Discussion → Project Proposal / Archive
```

### Status Definitions
- Idea – Newly proposed topic
- Discussion – Under active community discussion: Assessing relevance, scope, or feasibility
- Workstream - Active workstream inside the Business Operations Study Group
- Project Proposal – Candidate for a dedicated project or workstream
- Archived – No longer actively pursued
